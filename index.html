One ordinary billing month

Every scene below is something that happens at a subscription company. Here is what Brightloop does for each one.

A new customer signs up

One account per email. Passwords need more than eight characters with upper case, lower case and a special character. A reset link goes to the registered email.

A new plan launches

The admin adds a product with sales price, cost and variants (choosing the Odoo brand adds ₹560), then a plan that bills daily, weekly, monthly or yearly with a minimum quantity and availability dates.

A quote becomes a subscription

Reps start from a quotation template with the plan and lines already in, valid for a set number of days. Every subscription has a number and moves from Draft to Quotation to Confirmed to Active to Closed.

Billing day

One click creates every due invoice with customer, lines, tax and totals filled in. Each billing period has a unique key, so nothing is billed twice or skipped.

A customer asks for a better price

Admins set fixed or percentage discounts with minimum spend or seats, dates and a usage limit. The best match lands on the next invoice by itself and stops when it expires or runs out.

The money comes in

Customers pay through the Resso Pay checkout, and staff can record cash, cheque or bank transfers with a note. Each payment lands on its invoice, and paid or outstanding is always visible.

Month end, in one screen

Active subscriptions, monthly recurring revenue, money collected, money still owed and overdue invoices, with date filters. Answers that took days now take seconds.

Customers manage themselves

A portal shows each customer only their own subscriptions and invoices. They can accept a quote, pause or close a plan when allowed, and pay without emailing anyone.

Built carefully with money in mind

Strong passwords. Length and character rules, hashed storage.
Safe resets. Single-use token that expires in 15 minutes.
Roles on every action. Not just hidden buttons.
Own data only. Customers can’t see anyone else’s records.
No double billing. Unique key for each subscription and period.
Escaped input. Protects against script injection.

Questions people ask

Can I try it without signing up?

Yes. The demo app has ready-made admin, staff and customer logins, listed in the README.

Is Resso Pay a real payment gateway?

It is a realistic simulation for the demo: card, UPI and net banking, with an OTP step. No money moves. A live gateway would plug into the same place.

How do discounts get applied?

When an invoice is created, Brightloop checks every active discount. If one applies, the best one is added automatically and its use count goes up.

Where is my data kept?

In this demo, in your own browser. A production version stores it in a database behind a secure server.

Will it stay fast with thousands of subscriptions?

The design uses indexed lookups, filtered lists and pagination so pages stay under two seconds.
